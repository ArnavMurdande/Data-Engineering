### 💡 Pro-Tips for Maximum Cinematic Polish:
1. **PowerPoint Morph Setup:** After Copilot generates the slides, go to the **Transitions** tab, select all slides, and click **Morph**. When duplicate elements (like map pins or state icons) appear on successive slides, Morph will automatically animate them smoothly.
2. **Foreground Cutouts:** If Copilot gives you flat, boxy photos, right-click the image of the monument or dancer, choose **Picture Format $\rightarrow$ Remove Background**, and place it slightly over the title text. This creates the 3D Instagram/Reels cutout look instantly.
3. **Audio Tip:** Pair your team presentation with a very faint ambient backing track (e.g., subtle sitar/flute drone) played at 5% volume for an auditorium-grade docuseries feel.
